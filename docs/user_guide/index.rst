**********
User Guide
**********

The Interaction EDA step decomposes the interaction energy between the fragments of a
cluster -- usually its molecules -- into electrostatics, Pauli repulsion, dispersion,
polarization and charge transfer. It computes the whole cluster and its pairs, and
optionally its triples, and splits each term into its 2-body and many-body parts.
